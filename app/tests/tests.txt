# Tests
## Test 1 - Landing page loads at root [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Description: The root route shows the landing page with navigation tabs, not the scanner.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Wait for the page to fully load
3. Look for the header brand and the navigation tabs
Expected: The 'Restaurant Bookkeeper' header with 'Home', 'Daybook', 'Bank', 'Reports', and 'Invoice Scanner' tabs is visible, plus landing cards for the Daybook and the Invoice Scanner; no disclaimer card is shown on the landing page.
## Test 2 - Scan control gated until consent
Viewport: mobile
Description: On the scanner tab, the scan control stays disabled until the disclaimer checkbox is checked (guardrail).
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app, wait for load, and tap the 'Invoice Scanner' tab
2. Observe the 'Choose invoice photo' control before checking anything
3. Check the 'I understand and accept' checkbox
Expected: Before checking, the 'Choose invoice photo' control appears disabled (grey, not-allowed cursor); after checking, it appears enabled with gold styling.
## Test 3 - Recent scans section renders on scanner tab
Viewport: desktop
Description: The scanner tab's history section renders, showing either stored scans or the empty state.
Steps:
1. Navigate to the app
2. Wait for the page to fully load
3. Open the 'Invoice Scanner' tab and scroll to the 'Recent scans' section
Expected: The 'Recent scans' heading is visible with either a list of scanned invoices or the text 'No scans yet.'
